How To Play

GAME OBJECTIVE

Build your fictional game-bank balance and reach $1,000,000,000,000 (1 Trillion) while having no more than $1.01 in debt.

HOW THE GAME WORKS

  • Choose a custom entry fee (Minimum entry fee is $100).
  • Pay the entry fee using fictional game money.
  • If you don't have enough cash, borrow fictional money subject to borrowing rules.
  • Choose your personal briefcase to keep.
  • Open briefcases each round to eliminate prizes from the board.
  • The Banker makes cash offers after each round based on remaining risk and EV.
  • Choose DEAL to accept the offer or NO DEAL to keep playing.

CASES & BANKER

There are always 26 cases containing unique prizes. The Banker calculates offers using remaining briefcase values, current round, risk, and expected value.

FICTIONAL BANK

The Bank is part of the game's fictional economy. It is NOT a real bank.

  • All money, loans, debt, and interest are entirely fictional.
  • There are no real-money transactions, deposits, withdrawals, or real prizes.

BORROWING & INTEREST

  • Maximum outstanding principal borrowed limit: $25,000.
  • Borrowing increases cash and debt by exactly the amount borrowed.
  • A 5.0% interest rate is charged on outstanding debt when starting a game.

DEBT LIMIT & PAYMENTS

If your total debt reaches $30,000 or more, you go bankrupt and lose immediately.

You can pay off debt anytime in the Bank view using your available cash.

MILESTONES & FINAL WIN

Milestones celebrate your cash achievements (e.g. $100k, $1M, $1B) and do NOT end the game.

Final Victory Requirement: $1,000,000,000,000 Cash and $\le$ $1.01 Debt.

✔ $1,000,000,000,000 + $0.00 debt → WIN

✔ $1,000,000,000,000 + $1.01 debt → WIN

❌ $1,000,000,000,000 + $1.02 debt → NOT A WIN

❌ $999,999,999,999 + $0.00 debt → NOT A WIN

Game Settings & Reset

Goal: Reach $1,000,000,000,000 cash with $1.01 or less debt.

Resetting will completely wipe all progress, saved balances, debt, and history.
